# ADR 0015: Drift spatial HUD ergonomics, guided work stepper, tactile lens pill, and in-place FocusOverlay stepping

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Track:** `drift-navigation_20261114`

## Context

Completing the 3-track sociological wayfinding overhaul ([ADR 0014](./0014-sociological-ux-survey-and-wayfinding-architecture.md)) required resolving four empirical friction points inside **The Drift** (`src/components/DriftIsland.astro`, `src/world/**`):

1. **Mobile and touch-tablet HUD collisions and viewport occlusion:** On `pointer: coarse` devices exposing `DeviceOrientationEvent`, `TiltControl` reveals `[data-drift-tilt]` (*"Move with the phone"* / *"Bouger avec le téléphone"*) inside `.drift-top`, colliding with permanent `.drift-help` paragraphs across `20,580–21,120 px²` on iPhones and `54–4,489 px²` on `820×1180` tablets, while wrapped `.drift-zones` pills stacked up to `4` rows in French (`55.11%` viewport coverage on `fr:375×667`).
2. **Undiscoverable progression and optical lenses:** Advancing work-by-work or cycling the six camera techniques required 3D drag/raycast skill or memorised shortcuts (`Tab`, `L`, or a 500 ms motionless touch hold).
3. **Sparse `FocusOverlay` inspection without in-place stepping:** Opening `[data-drift-focus]` showed only the title and poetic fragment (omitting dimensions, year, medium, non-commercial availability status, and `i / N` sequence context), rendered `[data-focus-work]` / `[data-focus-contact]` at `20 px` height, and forced visitors to close and re-drag to inspect adjacent works.
4. **Strict architectural invariants to preserve:** UX Principle 1 (*"Drift, don't navigate"*), ADR 0006 (`100%` unit coverage outside `src/world/gl/`), `Tab × 3` keyboard path (`Mute` → `Wake up` → nearest work in `[data-drift-works]`), exact `[data-drift-lens]` live-region markup, `?kiosk=1` zero-link exhibition mode ([ADR 0012](./0012-leak-specimens-and-exhibition-mode.md)), `LanguageGuard`, `CopyGuard`, non-commercial presentation, and `≤ 8 KB` gz boot / `≤ 250 KB` gz world JS budgets.

## Decision

- **Pure island controllers and manifest curatorial fields outside `src/world/gl/` under 100% Vitest + `fast-check` coverage (`src/world/core/`, `src/world/manifest/`, `src/world/island/`):**
  - `WorldManifest` and `WorldManifestBuilder` enrich `ManifestWork` with localized `metaLine`, non-commercial `availabilityLabel`, `contextLabel`, and 1-based `positionIndex` / `positionTotal` computed in deterministic `FieldLayout` stop order so sequence counters step monotonically (`1 / 40 → 2 / 40 → …`) in lockstep with `DriftSession.stops`.
  - `DriftHelp` (`src/world/island/DriftHelp.ts`) selects pointer-aware onboarding copy (`data-hint-coarse` vs `data-hint-fine`), holds its timer while `ThresholdOverlay` or `FocusOverlay` is up, auto-dismisses `[data-drift-onboarding]` after `≥ 2.5 m` of camera displacement or `≥ 6 s` of active drifting, and manages `[data-drift-help-toggle]` (`aria-expanded`) + `[data-drift-help-panel]` (dismissible via button click or `Escape`, returning focus to the toggle without intercepting `Escape` inside `<dialog>`).
  - `WorkStepper` (`src/world/island/WorkStepper.ts`) drives `[data-drift-prev]`, `[data-drift-current]` (`<Title> · i / N` with ellipsis-safe title span and fixed sequence span, opening `FocusOverlay` on click/tap), and `[data-drift-next]`, persisting any stepper-initiated glide target while `DriftSession` glides so rapid multi-click stepping advances cleanly without snapping back to the departing work's nearest-neighbour reading.
  - `LensToggle` (`src/world/island/LensToggle.ts`) + `LensInput.aimAtFacing()` drive `[data-lens-cycle]`, centering aim on the faced work when clicked outside the canvas, showing the work's curated default lens on first un-hovered activation (or advancing immediately via `LensDirector.cycle` / `LensSession.cycle` when already viewed), and synchronizing with `LensAnnouncer` (`[data-drift-lens]`).
  - `FocusOverlay` (`src/world/island/FocusOverlay.ts`) renders `[data-focus-context]` (`Dreamland · Arrival · i / 40`), `[data-focus-meta]` (`metaLine · availabilityLabel`), and in-place `<button type="button">` stepping (`[data-focus-prev]`, `[data-focus-next]`, plus `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight`), gliding the camera via `session.focus`, updating `?w=<slug>` via `history.replaceState` outside `?kiosk=1`, avoiding duplicate `showModal()` calls while open, and omitting `href` attributes in `?kiosk=1` so `a[href]` count stays strictly `0`.
- **Collision-free responsive HUD and single-row horizontal-scroll zone strip (`src/components/DriftIsland.astro`):**
  - Place `[data-drift-onboarding]` strictly below `.drift-top` (`top: 4rem` vs `.drift-top` `8..52 px`), compact mobile `.drift-top` buttons so all three touch controls fit on one `44 px` row even in French at `320–375 px`, place `.drift-dock` and `[data-drift-help-panel]` strictly **after `[data-drift-works]`** in DOM order (`Tab × 3` preserved), hide the decorative `.drift-horizon` line below `640 px`, and style `.drift-zones` with `flex-wrap: nowrap; overflow-x: auto; touch-action: pan-x` plus auto-margin safe centering so `zoneStripRowCount === 1` and HUD chrome coverage stays `≤ 25%` across every viewport (`24.46%` on `fr:375×667`, `19.17%` on `fr:390×844`, `12.63%` on `fr:820×1180`).
  - Enforce `≥ 44×44 px` (`2.75rem`) minimum target boxes across all `.drift` buttons and `FocusOverlay` action pills (`[data-focus-work]`, `[data-focus-contact]`).

## Considered & Rejected Alternatives

- **Wrap top and bottom HUD bars inside partial-height `pointer-events: none` containers.** Rejected: `ThresholdOverlay` marks direct children of `[data-drift]` `inert` except `.drift-top` and `[data-threshold]`, and `collectDriftHudRects` measures non-fullscreen containers by their outer bounding box (which would falsely inflate measured chrome coverage with empty transparent space). Keeping `.drift-top`, `[data-drift-onboarding]`, `.drift-dock`, and `.drift-compass` as compact sibling children preserves `ThresholdOverlay` inert scoping and exact HUD coverage geometry.
- **Keep `.drift-zones` `flex-wrap: wrap` above `640 px`.** Rejected: French zone labels on `820×1180` tablets wrapped onto `2` rows in Track 14 telemetry; applying `flex-wrap: nowrap; overflow-x: auto; touch-action: pan-x` universally with safe auto-margins centers pills on wide monitors while keeping `1` scrollable row on phones and tablets.
- **Let `[data-lens-cycle]` call `LensSession.cycle()` without aiming at the faced work.** Rejected: when a mouse-only or touch visitor clicks `[data-lens-cycle]` in the bottom dock, the pointer is outside the 3D canvas (`lens.slug === null`), making a bare `lens.cycle()` call a silent no-op. Pairing `LensInput.aimAtFacing()` with `LensDirector` / `LensSession` activates and cycles all optical shaders directly on the faced work.
- **Use `<a href="?w=...">` links for `WorkStepper` or `FocusOverlay` `Prev / Next` controls.** Rejected: triggers full document reloads (dropping WebGL and WebAudio state) and breaks the `?kiosk=1` zero-link invariant; `<button type="button">` driving `DriftSession.focus` + `history.replaceState` keeps transitions seamless and link-free.
