# ADR 0014: Sociological visitor UX audit engine and cross-layer wayfinding architecture

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Track:** `ux-sociological-survey_20261114`

## Context

`iknowyoucanseeme.xyz` serves seven sociologically distinct visitor segments—contemporary art curators (`curator`), mobile social followers (`instagram-mobile`), collectors (`collector`), fellow photographers and ceramists (`peer-artist`), physical gallery QR visitors (`gallery-qr`), non-gamer museum visitors (`non-gamer`), and keyboard-only or motion-sensitive visitors (`keyboard-a11y`).

Live Chromium telemetry across `375×667`, `390×844`, `820×1180`, `1440×900`, and `1920×1080` in English and French (`docs/ux/sociological-survey.md`) exposed measurable layout and wayfinding gaps:

1. **Mobile and tablet HUD collisions and occlusion:** `.drift-top` (including `[data-drift-tilt]` on touch contexts) and `.drift-help` share `top: 1rem` without collision avoidance, overlapping by `20,580–21,120 px²` on iPhones and `54–4,489 px²` on `820×1180` tablets, while wrapped `.drift-zones` pills push total French HUD chrome coverage to `55.11%` on `375×667`.
2. **Broken cross-layer continuity:** Non-Home Awake routes lack a 1-click Drift entry when `iknowyoucanseeme:mode === 'awake'` (`2` clicks required), and clicking `Wake up` from a deep-linked work (`/?w=<slug>`) drops context on Awake Home when that work is not one of the three static teasers.
3. **Irreversible Awake filtering and sparse work/territory orientation:** Clicking `#dreamland` on `WorksIndexPage` hides *The border is thin* (`28 / 40` works) with no 1-click reset or photography-vs-ceramics filter; `WorkPage` lacks top breadcrumbs, `i / N` sequence context, and visual `prev / next` thumbnails; `TerritoryPage` lacks `#zone-*` jump links, Drift portals, and sibling-territory continuation; standalone Awake and `FocusOverlay` links render at `18–21 px` height (`< 24 px` WCAG 2.5.8 AA and `< 44 px` coarse comfort).
4. **Undiscoverable Drift stepping and inspection:** Advancing work-by-work or cycling the six camera lenses requires 3D drag/raycast skill or memorised shortcuts (`Tab`, `L`, 500 ms motionless touch hold), and `FocusOverlay` omits curatorial metadata and in-dialog `Previous / Next` stepping.

Every solution must simultaneously obey UX Principle 1 (*"Drift, don't navigate"*), UX Principle 2 (*"Never truly lost"*), ADR 0006 (`100%` unit coverage outside `src/world/gl/`), ADR 0007 (`0 B` static-page JS budget in `DistGuard`), ADR 0009 (reduced motion does not route away from the world), ADR 0012 (`?kiosk=1` zero-link exhibition mode and Leak outer-margin clearance), and strict non-commercial presentation.

## Decision

- **Pure UX audit engine under 100% unit + `fast-check` coverage (`src/ux/`), paired with live Chromium telemetry (`tests/e2e/`).**
  - `VisitorPersonas` (`src/ux/VisitorPersonas.ts`) models all 7 visitor archetypes with explicit locales, viewports, pointer precision (`fine` vs `coarse`), spatial literacy, goals, and positive integer weights summing to `100`.
  - `NavigationAudit` (`src/ux/NavigationAudit.ts`) provides pure, deterministic geometry and UX evaluation: `detectRectOverlaps` (interior pairwise bounding-box intersections), `viewportCoverageRatio` (exact sweep-line rectangle union clipped to the viewport), `auditTouchTargets` (`24×24 px` WCAG 2.5.8 AA on `fine` pointers, `44×44 px` comfort on `coarse` pointers), `evaluateWayfindingChecks` (8 cross-site capabilities), and `scorePersona` / `explainPersonaScore` (verified monotonic by `fast-check`).
  - `uxSurveyCollector.ts` and `uxSurvey.spec.ts` (`tests/e2e/`) measure live Chromium DOM bounding boxes and interaction paths across EN and FR so Track 14 locks in empirical baseline telemetry (`12.45–22.91 / 100`) and Track 16 reuses the identical collector to prove post-overhaul persona scores `≥ 90 / 100`.
- **Awake layer wayfinding blueprint (governed for Track 15 `awake-navigation_20261114`).**
  - Align `.site-header` and `.site-footer` inner containers to `max-width: 72rem; margin: 0 auto`, upgrade target sizes (`≥ 24×24 px` universal, `≥ 44×44 px` on `pointer: coarse`), and add a persistent `[data-header-drift]` portal (`/{lang}/?w=<slug>` on work/territory pages, `/{lang}/?drift=1` elsewhere) backed by `DeepLink` + `DriftBoot` intent override so Drift is always 1 click away without violating the single-`iknowyoucanseeme` Home rule or adding `"search"` chrome.
  - Keep `WorksIndexPage` at **`0 B` JavaScript** (`DistGuard`) by pairing an `#all-works` reset anchor with native `<input type="radio" name="medium-filter">` + `<label>` pills styled and filtered purely via CSS `:has(:checked)`.
  - Add `.work-breadcrumb` (`i / N` sequence), a desktop `position: sticky` curatorial plinth, and thumbnail `.prev-next` cards on `WorkPage`; add `#zone-*` jump links, Drift portals, and cross-territory continuation on `TerritoryPage`; and surface a contextual `[data-awake-resume]` link beside `[data-drift-start]` on Awake Home whenever `?w=<slug>` is present after `Wake up`.
- **Drift spatial instrument wayfinding blueprint (governed for Track 16 `drift-navigation_20261114`).**
  - Replace permanent `.drift-help` paragraphs with a collision-free top layout, a pointer-aware transient `[data-drift-onboarding]` hint (`≥ 2.5 m` movement or `6 s`), and a toggleable `[data-drift-help-panel]` opened from `[data-drift-help-toggle]`.
  - Add a bottom wayfinding dock—placed **strictly after `[data-drift-works]` in DOM order** to preserve `Tab × 3`—housing `WorkStepper` (`[data-drift-prev]`, `[data-drift-current]`, `[data-drift-next]`), `LensToggle` (`[data-lens-cycle]`), and a single-row horizontal-scroll `.drift-zones` strip on narrow/tablet portrait screens.
  - Enrich `WorldManifest` and `FocusOverlay` with localized curatorial metadata (`[data-focus-meta]`: dimensions, year, medium, availability status) and in-dialog `<button type="button">` stepping (`[data-focus-prev]`, `[data-focus-next]`, `ArrowLeft` / `ArrowRight`) that glides the camera and syncs `?w=` via `replaceState` while preserving `?kiosk=1`'s zero-link QR behavior.

## Considered & Rejected Alternatives

- **Render a conventional site navigation bar (`Works / Exhibitions / About / Contact`) inside the 3D Drift world.** Rejected: violates UX Principle 1 (*"Drift, don't navigate. No conventional menu in the world; movement is exploration"*), turns the artwork into a framed widget, and introduces `<a href>` elements that `KioskMode` (`?kiosk=1`) would have to strip or special-case. `Wake up` + diegetic bottom instruments (`WorkStepper`, `LensToggle`, `HorizonCompass`) solve orientation without breaking immersion.
- **Ship a client-side JavaScript filter bundle on `WorksIndexPage` for territory and medium toggles.** Rejected: violates ADR 0007 and `DistGuard`'s `0 B` JavaScript budget on `/{lang}/works/`. Modern CSS `:target` reset anchors combined with `<input type="radio">` + `:has(:checked)` achieve instant, accessible, zero-JS reversible filtering that even works with browser scripts disabled.
- **Place bottom Drift stepper and lens buttons before `[data-drift-works]` in DOM order.** Rejected: `tests/e2e/drift.spec.ts` and WCAG keyboard ergonomics rely on `Tab × 3` (`Mute` → `Wake up` → nearest work in `[data-drift-works]`). Placing bottom dock buttons after `[data-drift-works]` matches visual top-to-bottom reading order and preserves `Tab × 3`.
- **Use `<a href="?w=...">` anchors for Drift `WorkStepper` or `FocusOverlay` `Prev / Next` controls.** Rejected: clicking `<a href>` triggers full document navigation (reloading WebGL and dropping audio gesture state) and violates the `?kiosk=1` zero-link invariant (`ADR 0012`); `<button type="button">` driving `DriftSession` + `history.replaceState` works smoothly in normal mode and stays link-free in kiosk mode.
- **Hardcode synthetic UX persona scores in unit fixtures without live browser DOM geometry.** Rejected: hardcoded numbers cannot catch emergent layout interactions such as `TiltControl` unhiding `[data-drift-tilt]` on `820×1180` touch tablets or 4-row French `.drift-zones` wrapping on `375×667` phones (`55.11%` viewport coverage). Computing scores from live Playwright `getBoundingClientRect()` snapshots makes both the Track 14 baseline and Track 16 `≥ 90 / 100` gate empirically falsifiable.
